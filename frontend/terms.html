1. What Is Desito AI Bot?

Desito AI Bot is a tool that helps business owners connect their Facebook Page to an automated bot. The bot can send automated Welcome Messages, Auto Replies to Messages, and Automatic Comment Replies based on the Knowledge / FAQ the business owner provides.

3. How The Service Works

  • After you connect your Page, you can set your Welcome Message, turn ON / OFF Auto Reply to Message, and turn ON / OFF Automatic Comment Reply.
  • The bot will reply to your customers using the Knowledge you provide in your Dashboard.
  • If a customer asks for a human, the bot will pause for that customer and notify you so a human can take over.
  • You can Disconnect your Page at any time from your Dashboard. This will stop the bot immediately.
