Privacy Policy for Desito AI Bot

Last Updated: November 12, 2026
Contact: support@desito.store - Enugu, Nigeria

1. What We Collect

When you login with Facebook and connect your Facebook Page, we collect:

  • Your Facebook Name and Facebook User ID
  • Your Facebook Page Name, Page ID, and Page Access Token
  • Messages and Comments sent to your Page by your customers, so the bot can reply
  • Your Welcome Message, Auto-Reply Settings, and Knowledge / FAQ you set in Dashboard

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to:

  • Connect your Facebook Page to our bot
  • Automatically reply to messages and comments on your Page using the Knowledge you provide
  • Show you your connected Pages in your Dashboard
  • Allow you to pause the bot when a human needs to take over (Handover)

We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT share your data with third parties, except as required by Meta / Facebook to send replies.

3. Data Storage

Your Page information and settings are stored securely on our server. Your Page Access Token is used only to send replies on your behalf to your Page.

4. How To Delete Your Data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Go to Dashboard and click Disconnect on your Page - this deletes your Page Token and Settings from our server immediately.
  • Or go to Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites > Remove Desito AI Bot - this revokes access.
  • Or email us at support@desito.store and we will delete all your data within 7 days.

5. Data Deletion Callback

If you request data deletion through Facebook, we will delete all data associated with your Facebook User ID and Page ID from our database.

6. Children

This service is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes To This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Last Updated date on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@desito.store
